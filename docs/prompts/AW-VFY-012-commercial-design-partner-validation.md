# AW-VFY-012 — Commercial Design-Partner Validation

Status: Ready only after `AW-VFY-011` passes the external-value gate
Type: commercial validation only

## Objective

Prove that a real buyer will fund the independent verification workflow before AgentsWatch invests in SaaS/team administration, billing, enterprise packaging or broad integrations.

Do not build billing or team infrastructure to execute this prompt.

## Required buyer discovery

For every serious buyer conversation record:
- user role;
- budget owner / payer role;
- team size;
- coding-agent stack;
- current verification/review workflow;
- validated pain from AW-VFY-011;
- purchasing trigger;
- procurement/security blockers;
- preferred packaging;
- tested price/package;
- concrete next step.

## Pricing hypotheses to test

Use adjacent developer-tool pricing only as context, not as proof.

Test at least two realistic packaging models, for example:
- team flat-rate pilot;
- per-seat team plan;
- enterprise/custom verification package.

Do not ask only "would you pay?".

Ask for one of:
- paid pilot;
- paid design-partner agreement;
- invoice-ready commitment;
- procurement/security review with agreed commercial next step;
- equivalent concrete buying commitment.

## Required output

1. buyer / payer matrix;
2. pain-to-budget mapping;
3. tested packaging/pricing table;
4. objections and procurement blockers;
5. concrete commercial commitments;
6. evidence separating user enthusiasm from buyer intent;
7. Gate 7 decision: PASS / FAIL / NARROW;
8. recommendation: minimal commercial packaging, reposition, or stop.

## Pass candidate

- >=3 external teams make paid-pilot/design-partner or equivalent concrete commercial commitments;
- budget owner is identified;
- verification/review/governance is the reason for payment;
- at least one pricing/package hypothesis survives real buyer discussion;
- the requested paid product remains consistent with AgentsWatch's independent verifier thesis.

## Stop / pivot signals

- all serious users expect permanent free use;
- buyer cannot identify a budget owner;
- commercial value depends on unrelated orchestration/runtime/dashboard features;
- the paid problem is better solved natively by the agent platform;
- support/security burden overwhelms plausible early revenue.

## Stop rules

- No SaaS/auth/billing implementation.
- No invented commitments.
- No counting a positive call as purchase intent.
- Do not promote commercial packaging unless evidence is recorded.
- If Gate 7 fails, update the decision record before any team/SaaS work.
